# Stage 08 – Integrated QC Review and Final Cell Filtering Specification

## Purpose

Integrate all upstream quality-control evidence into a single explicit filtering
decision for every cell.

Unlike earlier preprocessing stages, Stage 08 is responsible for deciding which
cells constitute the canonical dataset for downstream analyses.

The stage combines:

- adaptive QC metrics and thresholds from Stage 05;
- ambient RNA correction from Stage 06;
- doublet predictions from Stage 07.

No new QC metrics are calculated.

---

## Philosophy

Quality control should be treated as an evidence-integration problem rather than
a sequence of independent thresholding operations.

Individual QC signals frequently represent legitimate biology rather than
technical artifacts. Therefore, diagnostic evidence and applied removal criteria
are intentionally separated.

A QC flag indicates that a cell deserves attention.

A removal criterion indicates that sufficient evidence exists to exclude the
cell from downstream analyses.

## Pipeline decisions

### Diagnostic flags are distinct from removal criteria

The pipeline intentionally separates diagnostic evidence from filtering
decisions.

Examples:

- high mitochondrial percentage is a diagnostic flag;
- low complexity is a diagnostic flag;
- high UMI count is a diagnostic flag;
- high detected-gene count is a diagnostic flag.

These annotations remain available for downstream interpretation even when they
do not directly remove cells.

---

### Conservative mitochondrial filtering

High mitochondrial percentage alone is **not** considered sufficient evidence
for removal.

Rationale:

- mitochondrial expression varies among tissues;
- stress responses may be biologically relevant;
- metabolically active cell types can exhibit elevated mitochondrial
  transcription.

Instead, mitochondrial percentage contributes to filtering only when accompanied
by reduced transcriptomic complexity.

---

### Low transcriptomic complexity

Low complexity alone does not imply poor quality.

Specialized transcriptional programs may legitimately reduce complexity.

Therefore, low complexity is retained as diagnostic evidence unless additional
QC signals support removal.

---

### Doublets

Predicted doublets are removed.

Doublets represent composite transcriptomes that may distort:

- clustering;
- marker detection;
- trajectory inference;
- cell-cell communication;
- pseudobulk analyses.

---

### No embedding-driven QC

This stage intentionally avoids:

- PCA;
- UMAP;
- clustering.

Embedding geometry should never determine initial QC filtering because those
representations depend on downstream preprocessing choices.

Instead, QC metrics should later be projected onto embeddings for validation.

note: 
Prefer exclusion decisions supported by multiple complementary QC signals.

However, this is a guiding principle rather than an absolute rule.
Exceptionally severe QC abnormalities may justify exclusion when
supported by dataset-specific evidence of compromised cell quality.

QC metrics are not necessarily statistically independent.
For example, detected genes, total UMIs, and genes-per-UMI
complexity are mathematically related.


Exception: extreme mitochondrial enrichment

Very high mitochondrial fractions may indicate severely compromised
transcriptomes, particularly when accompanied by:

- very low detected-gene counts;
- low total UMIs;
- reduced transcriptome complexity;
- strong enrichment in a particular library;
- a distinct low-quality population in QC distributions.

Do not automatically apply a universal mitochondrial cutoff.

Instead, evaluate the extreme mitochondrial tail using the
observed QC distributions and determine whether additional
exclusion criteria are justified.

Document any dataset-specific thresholds and their rationale.


Critically, inspect both removed AND retained cells.

For retained cells:

- What fraction exceeds mitochondrial thresholds such as
  10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, and 50%?
- Are extremely high-mitochondrial cells also low-feature
  or low-UMI?
- Are these cells disproportionately concentrated in
  particular libraries?
- Does the proposed filtering policy leave a substantial
  population with clearly compromised transcriptomes?

If necessary, revise the filtering criteria and rerun Stage 08.

The initial filtered object should be considered provisional
until these checks are complete.
